Our work process

See how we complete the work

At Lasmurgreen, we believe that a beautiful outdoor space starts long before the first plant goes in the ground. Our tried and tested four-step process ensures every project runs smoothly, stays on budget and delivers results that exceed your expectation every single time.

Discuss plan

Every great garden begins with a great conversation. We take the time to site down with you understand your vision, lifestyle, and budget, and asses your outdoor space in detail. No rushing, No assumptions. just an open, honest discussion about what you want and how we can make it happen

Garden design

Once we understand your vision. Our talented design team gets to work creating a detailed personalised garden design tailored specifically to your space and lifestle. We blend creativity with horticultural expertise to produce a design that is not only beautiful but alos practical, sustainable and built to thrive in your local environment.

Planting

With the plan approved and materials sourced our professional team moves on to the most exciting stage bringing your garden to life. Using only premium, nursery grown plants and top-quality materials, we work with precision and care to intall every element exactly as designed, ensuring your garden looks stunning from day one

Finish the job

At Lasmurgreen, finishing the job means more than just packing up and leaving. We carry out a thorough site clean up, conduct a full quality inspection and walk you through every element of your completed outdoor space to make sure everything is exactly as you envisioned and that you are completely happy before we call it done.
