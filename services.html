Landscape design and installation

At Lasmurgreen, landscape design and installation is at the heart of everything we do. We combine artistic creativity with deep horticultural knowledge to design and build outdoor spaces that are as functional as they are breathtaking. Whether you're starting from scratch or reimagining an existing space, our team crafts a personalised landscape that reflects your personality, complements your property, and thrives in your local environment.

Lawn and hedge maintenence

A well-maintained lawn and perfectly shaped hedges are the backbone of any great garden. Lasmurgreen's professional maintenance team keeps your outdoor space looking immaculate all year round — whether it's a regular weekly cut, seasonal treatment, or a full hedge reshaping. We take pride in the details so you can sit back and enjoy a garden that always looks its best.

Increte / Polished Stamped Floor Installation

Elevate your outdoor and indoor surfaces with Lasmurgreen's expert increte and polished stamped floor installation service. This decorative concrete solution combines durability with stunning visual appeal, giving your floors the look of natural stone, tile, or brick — at a fraction of the cost. Perfect for driveways, patios, pool surrounds, and interior floors, our stamped finishes add a premium touch to any space.

Landscape Architecture

Landscape architecture goes beyond planting and maintenance — it is the art and science of thoughtfully designing outdoor environments that are beautiful, functional, and sustainable. Lasmurgreen's landscape architects bring a wealth of technical expertise and creative vision to every project, delivering master-planned outdoor spaces for residential estates, commercial properties, and public environments.

Patios / Stepstones / Walkways

A well-designed patio, stepstone, or walkway does more than connect spaces — it defines the character of your entire outdoor area. Lasmurgreen designs and installs stunning hardscape features using premium natural and engineered materials, creating pathways and outdoor living areas that are as durable as they are beautiful. Whether you want a sweeping garden walkway, a cosy patio retreat, or elegant stepping stones across your lawn, we bring craftsmanship and style to every project.

Gazebo / Hut

There is something truly special about having a beautiful outdoor structure to retreat to — a place to relax, entertain, or simply enjoy the beauty of your garden in comfort. Lasmurgreen designs and builds bespoke gazebos and garden huts that perfectly complement your outdoor space, adding elegance, shade, and a touch of luxury that transforms your garden into a true outdoor living destination.

Irrigation System

A healthy garden needs consistent, reliable water — and Lasmurgreen's professional irrigation systems make sure every plant, lawn, and flower bed gets exactly what it needs, exactly when it needs it. Our expertly designed and installed irrigation systems save you time, reduce water waste, and keep your garden looking lush and vibrant throughout every season — all at the touch of a button.

Indoor And Outdoor Plants

Plants have the power to transform any space — indoors or out — into something vibrant, calming, and full of life. Lasmurgreen supplies and installs a wide range of premium, carefully selected indoor and outdoor plants sourced from trusted nurseries and growers. Whether you need striking tropical houseplants to elevate your interior, or a colourful mix of garden plants to fill your outdoor space with life and colour, our plant specialists will help you choose and care for the perfect varieties.

Football Pitch Installation

Lasmurgreen brings the same precision and passion we put into our gardens to the installation of world-class football pitches. Whether for a school, community club, private estate, or commercial facility, we design and install high-performance football pitches built to meet professional standards — durable, safe, and beautiful to play on.

At Lasmurgreen, we believe every outdoor space has the potential to become somthingtruly extraordinary. From lush garden designs to expertly maintained lawns, our passionate team brings nature's beauty to your doorstep, crafted with care, delivered with pride
